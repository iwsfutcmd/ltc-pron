A colloquial layer for predict_yue

Wiktionary predicts one Cantonese reading for each Middle Chinese reading, and that reading is the literary one. These notes explain how the prediction is built, add a colloquial (白讀) layer as predict_yue_coll, fix four gaps in the literary layer, and test both against Wiktionary's attested readings and the words those readings occur in. Characters and words cited as examples link to their Wiktionary entries.

Readings of 重紐 finals carry a suffix such as -重鈕 (1,982 readings), which infer_categories folds into the division. Entering-tone finals are first renamed to their 入聲 rhyme (庚 → 陌 when the tone is 入).

infer_categories in Module:ltc-pron reduces the string to three integers: one of 38 initials, one of 160 final types (final × division × openness, as far as each final distinguishes them), and a tone from 1 to 4. Both predictors, predict_cmn and predict_yue, take only those three numbers.

How predict_yue builds a syllable

The function works in an internal notation where capital letters mark vowel quality and length, and only spells the result out in Jyutping at the end. It runs five stages, in this order. The colloquial layer hooks into three of them, and three literary fixes sit in the rhyme stage; both are marked below.

  1. Initial

    initial_outcomes_yue maps the 38 initials to Jyutping onsets. Overrides follow in priority order:

    • Labials become f before the 16 finals that caused 輕唇化 (e.g. 分 fan1).
    • Voiced obstruents in the level tone devoice to aspirates: b→p, d→t, z→c, g→k (平 ping4).
    • 溪 k becomes h before the 146 finals in debuccalisation_inducing_yue (客 hak1, 開 hoi1). Several initials become j before front glides.
    Literary fix. Fourteen finals left the debuccalisation list, so 溪 keeps k there and the rounding rule in stage 3 gives kw: 區 keoi1, 困 kwan3, 誇 kwaa1.
    Colloquial hook 1. Devoicing to an aspirate also applies when a fully voiced initial meets the rising tone (全濁上), giving 重 cung5 instead of zung6.

  2. Rhyme

    final_class_yue folds the 160 final types into 40 rhyme classes. Classes 1–20 have one outcome. Classes 21–34 have three outcomes, chosen by place of articulation (labial, coronal, velar/laryngeal). In 模 that gives 布 bou3, 都 dou1, 古 gu2. Classes 35–40 have eight outcomes, one per initial class. Open 支脂之微 give 比 bei2, 地 dei6, 知 zi1, 子 zi2, 機 gei1, 衣 ji1. Seven special cases come first, e.g. 疑 + 模 gives the syllabic 五 ng5.

    Literary fixes. Three of the seven special cases are new: 眞 after 來 and 精組 (鄰 leon4), 月合 and 乏 after voiced labials (罰 fat6), and 緝 after 從 and 邪 (集 zaap6).

  3. Onset adjustments from the rhyme

    疑 ng becomes j before high vowels. Before the 60 rounded (合口) finals, h becomes f, a zero or j onset becomes w, and g/k become gw/kw, unless the rhyme vowel is already rounded.

    Colloquial hook 2. After these adjustments, five rhyme replacements: 梗攝 II aN→AN, open 梗攝 III/IV IN→eN, 德 aN→AN, 怗 im→Am, and 脂 after 心 i→ei. Placing them here keeps 疑 as j before a former high vowel: 迎 jing4 → jeng4.

  4. Tone

    The four Middle Chinese tones split by voicing. A voiceless entering tone splits again by vowel length: long vowels go to tone 3 (中入), short vowels to tone 1. Voiced entering is tone 6. A fully voiced initial in the rising tone moves to tone 6 (濁上變去). Other voiced syllables add 3 (陽平 4, 陽上 5, 陽去 6).

    Colloquial hook 3. 全濁上 keeps 陽上 tone 5. The long-vowel test also counts e, so colloquial 尺 comes out cek3. 德 is the exception: its lowered vowel keeps tone 1 (黑 haak1), so that rule sets a short_entering flag that skips the test.

  5. Spell-out

    om merges into am. Final -m becomes -n after labial onsets (凡 faan4). In the entering tone the nasal codas become stops (-m -n -N → -p -t -k). The internal letters are then spelled in Jyutping:

The colloquial layer

The body of predict_yue is now a local function, yue_reflex(initial, final, tone, colloquial). predict_yue calls it with colloquial = false. The new predict_yue_coll calls it both ways and returns the colloquial form only when it differs, so a display can leave the cell empty where the two layers agree.

-- Colloquial (白讀) layer: 梗攝 rimes keep a low vowel, giving 2nd-division
-- -aang/-aak (生 saang1, 白 baak6) and open 3rd/4th-division -eng/-ek
-- (病 beng6, 石 sek6) beside literary -ang/-ak and -ing/-ik.
local colloquial_geng_2_yue = { [109] = true, [110] = true, [113] = true, [114] = true,
	[117] = true, [118] = true, [119] = true, [120] = true }
local colloquial_geng_34_yue = { [111] = true, [115] = true, [121] = true,
	[123] = true, [125] = true, [127] = true }

local function yue_reflex(initial, final, tone, colloquial)
	local initial_result = initial_outcomes_yue[initial]
	local voiced_rising = colloquial and tone == 2 and fully_voiced_initial[initial]	…
	elseif (tone == 1 or voiced_rising) and devoicing_initial_yue[initial] then	…
	local short_entering = false

	if colloquial then
		if colloquial_geng_2_yue[final] and final_result == "aN" then
			final_result = "AN"
		elseif colloquial_geng_34_yue[final] and final_result == "IN" then
			final_result = "eN"
		-- 德: 黑 haak1, 刻 haak1, 肋 laak6.
		elseif final == 131 and final_result == "aN" then
			final_result = "AN"
			short_entering = true
		-- 怗: 疊 daap6, 頰 gaap3, 莢 gaap3.
		elseif final == 158 and final_result == "im" then
			final_result = "Am"
		-- 脂 after 心 only: 四 sei3, 死 sei2, 肆 sei3.
		elseif final == 15 and initial == 16 and final_result == "i" then
			final_result = "ei"
		end
	end	…
		elseif not short_entering and mw.ustring.find(final_result, "^[AiouOYe]") ~= nil then	…
	elseif tone == 2 and fully_voiced_initial[initial] and not voiced_rising then	…
end

function export.predict_yue(initial, final, tone)
	return yue_reflex(initial, final, tone, false)
end

-- Expected colloquial (白讀) Cantonese reflex, or nil where the colloquial
-- layer is not expected to differ from predict_yue.
function export.predict_yue_coll(initial, final, tone)
	local colloquial = yue_reflex(initial, final, tone, true)
	if colloquial ~= yue_reflex(initial, final, tone, false) then
		return colloquial
	end
end

Are these forms colloquial?

Matching a reading says nothing about its register. For that, the readings were traced into words. Some 93,000 multi-character entries on Wiktionary have a Cantonese reading that aligns one syllable per character, giving 240,868 character tokens. An entry with a Cantonese reading but no Mandarin one is almost always Cantonese-specific vocabulary (生仔, 夜晚黑); 7.4% of tokens are in such vernacular words.

If a rule describes a colloquial layer, the words that use its form should be more vernacular than the words that use the literary form.

Every rule in predict_yue_coll passes: its forms sit in vernacular words between two and five and a half times as often as the literary forms. Counting characters rather than tokens, so that 生's hundreds of words do not dominate, the colloquial form is the more vernacular one for 31 梗攝 III–IV characters against 7, for 12 against 4 in 梗攝 II, and for 11 against 4 under 全濁上. 德 passes on tokens (driven by 黑) but splits 2 to 3 by character, so it is the weakest of the six.

This settles the 全濁上 question. The tone-5 aspirates are the only reading for many characters (舅 kau5, 婢 pei5), yet they behave as a colloquial layer: 10.0% vernacular against 3.9% for tone 6.

Frequency is not etymology, though. In 梗攝 II the colloquial forms outnumber the literary ones in words, 2,974 tokens to 2,250, and in voiceless entering syllables -aak is attested 59 times against 9 for -ak. The literary layer still keeps -ang/-ak, because that is the reflex the regular sound change gives; with both forms shown in the table (sang1 / saang1), nothing is lost by it.

The rejected candidates fail the other way. 緝 -aap is the ordinary reading in learned words such as 學習 and 集中, so after 從 and 邪 it became a literary fix instead. 豪 -aau is almost entirely one phonetic series (考 栲 洘 丂), and 咍 -aai is scattered across initials (乃 naai5, 孩 haai4, 貸 taai3) with no conditioning. Four further candidates have too few word tokens to judge: 日 n- (餌 nei6), 疑 ng- (蟻 ngai5), 蕭宵 -eu (調 deu6) and 歌戈 -oe (多 doe1).

When two rules meet one reading

Each colloquial rule changes one part of the syllable. The 梗攝 rules change the rhyme (-ing → -eng, -ang → -aang). The 全濁上 rule changes the onset and tone (unaspirated tone 6 → aspirated tone 5). Most readings meet the conditions of at most one rule, but a 梗攝 syllable with a fully voiced initial in the rising tone meets both. 挺 is one: 定 initial, 青 final, rising tone.

predict_yue_coll applies every rule whose conditions a reading meets, so for 挺 it changes the rhyme and the onset and tone, and returns teng5. That is what "stacking" means here. It treats the colloquial layer as one package: a word that entered Cantonese through the colloquial layer should show every colloquial change its syllable is eligible for. The alternative is that the changes are independent, so a word can have the colloquial rhyme without the colloquial tone, or the other way round.

Open questions

  1. Is the 溪 list fit for upstream?

    It gains 88 readings for 3 lost, but it records which words kept k-, not a sound law. The full evidence, a dead j- rule found along the way, and the options are in The 溪 initial in Cantonese. The recommendation there is to return to h-/f- everywhere and make the j- rule reachable.

  2. Can the thin candidates be tested another way?

    日 n-, 疑 ng-, 蕭宵 -eu and 歌戈 -oe each have 12 or fewer word tokens. The dial-pron Guangzhou column lists literary readings first and might separate them, though it covers only 1,023 characters.

How this was measured

The Lua modules run unchanged under Lua 5.1 (the version Scribunto uses) through lupa, with a small stand-in for mw.ustring and mw.text written in Python. Lua's own patterns match bytes, so the stand-in translates patterns to Python regular expressions and matches whole characters. Before any change, the local output was compared with what Wiktionary renders for 生 重 白 石 四 坐 話 吃 and agreed on every reading.

Data comes from the enwiktionary dump of 1 November 2026: 19,843 characters with Middle Chinese modules, 183,342 pages with {{zh-pron}}, 42,088 single-character pages with component data, and 1,027 dial-pron modules. Module data is evaluated in an empty Lua environment, so it can only build tables. Every change to predict.lua was measured with scripts/yue_compare.py and committed separately. Together with the change to the Cantonese cell in Module:ltc-pron, the diff against the live modules is 90 lines added and 20 removed.
